## Echo Cave (Lab, 21 Oct 2026)
- `reports/2026-10-21-arcade-echo-cave.html`: self-contained portrait exploration game, listed only in Lab through `dn:badge` "In development". Six selectable campaign caves unlock on completion; the daily layout uses a seeded London date. A round is one cave attempt, a level is its campaign cave number (daily: level 6, mode `daily`).
- Drag anywhere to walk (a short drag sneaks, a long drag runs), tap Echo (arrows/WASD run, Shift sneaks, Space echoes on desktop). Echoes propagate through connected ground and draw creatures to their origin. Walked ground remains faintly visible. No countdown; stars reward escaping with the relic, all crystals, then all hearts.
- Save key `digi-echo-cave-v1` holds stars, bests, daily results, settings and an unfinished expedition. Reload offers Continue expedition and opens a new tracked `resume` round; ordinary pause/resume keeps its round. Filenames, slug and save key stay stable on revision.
- Review update: explored-ground map pauses play and remembers discovered landmarks; the player explicitly taps Leave cave at the exit. Creature contact works while they wait, each sound is heard once, and visited ground does not expose live creatures through darkness. Touch steering preserves speed along shallow wall contacts (up to 35 degrees), gently guides narrow entrances, and uses rounded player contact with small diagonal cuts in exposed rock corners. Collision matches the drawn cuts; the seeded route grid is retained.
- The primary home button resumes an unfinished expedition. Optional v1 save fields retain echo recharge/waves, creature routes/search/stun/patrol state and discovery. Old saves still load; the date and layout of an existing daily attempt survive midnight retries. Stars and best records merge with current storage so an older tab cannot erase a completed result. The game still has one unfinished-expedition slot.
- v1.2 (superseded by v2 below for creatures): three luminous echo ripples travel through connected passages using a continuous acoustic distance field; Reduced motion uses a fading wash. Creatures spot the player within the 2.3-tile lamp range when rock does not block sight, warn for 0.42 seconds, then pursue at 2.9 tiles/sec (player: 2.6). They investigate the last seen position after sight breaks; a subsequent echo can redirect them while the player is hidden. Patrol and sound investigation are quicker, with contact recovery retained. New optional save fields preserve pursuit/reaction state; old saves load.

## Inkline (5 Nov 2026)
- `reports/2026-11-05-arcade-inkline.html`: a draw-to-solve physics Arcade game, portrait, in the Lab (`dn:badge` In development). One continuous line, no ink limit; lifting the finger turns it solid and releases the marble. A line through one peg pivots, through two it locks, otherwise it drops.
- **Hand-made levels** (6 Nov 2026, Alec: the bot-tuned levels started too low and lacked sensible, intuitive solutions): 10 levels in two chapters, Ledges (Mind the Gap, Slide, Backboard, Over the Wall, Gap and Wall) and Pegs (Lock, Pick Two, Hinge, Swing, Ski Jump). With the smooth ends (later on 6 Nov), Swing's peg moved to (128,138) with a shorter shelf, so the hammer meets the marble before the shelf's corner, and Ski Jump's wall rose 18 px, so a stick dropped in the bowl no longer kicks the marble over it. Each is built around one idea with the marble starting high, and checked by bot. Source, tools and design rules: `toolchain/inkline/` (README). **Chapters 3 to 5** (7 Nov 2026, same process): Big Air (Launch Pad, Updraft, Against the Flow, Curtain), Hands Off (Red Zone, Drop It, Drop the Ramp, Drawbridge) and Odd Inks (Trampoline, Bank Shot, Handle With Care), 21 levels in all. Later on 7 Nov: Two Gaps and Peak (Ledges), Hang Down and Landing Drop (Hands Off), Glass Steps (Odd Inks), 26 in all; then Sky Slide (Pegs) and Red Peak (Hands Off), 28. Levels can now go in mid-chapter: saves keep stars, fails and progress by level name (`save.ord`), and `gems.json` must be moved to the new positions too. Clockwork (moving scenery, `mv`) is still to be redone. The physics supports springs (`sp`), speed strips (`bo`, which also push sideways or up: an updraft), no-draw zones (`z`), inks and moving scenery. The level screen says "More pages are being drawn." Saves are `v:3`: stars from the old levels were reset (Daily bests kept).
- **Round:** one drawn line, from pen down to the marble reaching the cup or being lost. Touching the page during or after a run rewinds and starts the next line straight away, so an unfinished run sends no `game_end`. `level` is the level number (1 in the Daily), `mode` is `levels` or `daily`, `score` is the line in cm.
- **Daily:** every level and its mirror image (40 that pass; strips, pads and red zones are mirrored too), rotating by London day (`DAILY`), scored by the shortest line that reaches the cup, against the bot's shortest (`par`). No Board entry yet.
- **Physics:** matter-js 0.19.0 is inlined; fixed 1/120 s step and `Common._nextId` reset, so a line replays identically on one device. Local rules: line inertia computed by hand over the line's own parts (Matter adds the parent to the parts list); lines heavy (density 0.008) and grippy against scenery (0.9; the marble's 0.02 wins in marble pairs); scenery rounded (`chamfer`); a one-peg pivot is `pin()`, not a Matter constraint (it drifted about 20 px with multi-part lines and swung the wrong way); a pinned line's hinge has a little friction (`HINGE` 0.004 a step) so a swinging line settles; pegs no longer collide with the marble and catch a line within `PEGR` 15 px; a pinned line resting on scenery well away from its peg (over 30 px) holds its angle until it is knocked (Matter cannot hold it there by friction, so it crept off its support; a line touching only near its peg stays free, so a line drawn upright from a peg falls like a drawbridge); every piece of a line carries its ink's grip and bounce, and gets them back after locking (until 7 Nov a locked line lost them, so locked rubber never bounced); free rubber barely bounces, because the heavy line gives under the marble, so rubber levels lock it with pegs; glass breaks at any first touch faster than 4, the marble's or its own landing; low cups (`c[3]=1`) have two short walls.
- **Feel** (5 Nov 2026): gravity 1.3, top speed `TOP`, calmer bounce (marble 0.22, scenery 0.15) since 6 Nov; marble trail, wind in the air, landing dust and shake, Big air! pops; spring pads and speed strips.
- **Smoothing** (5 Nov 2026, Alec's idea): none while drawing, so no lag. On lift, `smooth` removes wobble by at most 2.5 px, keeps corners and anything touching a peg or object, and the line eases into shape over about 110 ms before the marble goes. Peg capture uses the stroke as drawn.
- **Drawing round things** (5 Nov 2026, Alec's call after first play): the ink never passes through a solid, the marble or a no-draw zone. Each 2 px step heads for the finger, or takes the free direction closest to it up to 80 degrees off, so it slides along edges and wraps corners; a finger put down on an object starts right at its nearest free edge, top side first. A step must bring the pen nearer the finger, so a finger pressed into an object leaves the pen resting on its surface rather than zigzagging there (that made a bump at a line's start or end). A dotted pencil line joins the ink to the finger while it slides, the pen sound drops, and clearance is 2.2 px.
- **Smooth line ends** (6 Nov 2026, Alec: "the line itself needs to always have a start and end that is smooth for the ball to go onto"): each end is a wedge, flat underneath and sloping on top from 0.6 px to full thickness over `TAPER` 56 px (a third of a short line), so the marble rolls on from either end without a lip. The wedge follows the line round its bends and is drawn as one outline. On lift, any part of the line closer to a surface than its own half-thickness moves out to rest on it (`clearOf`), so a line laid along a floor is not born buried and shoved out (that threw the marble about 19 px). A free line that stops just short of a surface it is heading into is carried on straight until it touches (`reachEnds`), so it lands seated instead of tipping; a pegged line's end that still falls short bends onto the surface, the shift fading out over 40 px (`settleEnds`). Test: `toolchain/inkline/endtest.js`.
- Saves: `digi-inkline-v1` (format `v:3`). `window.__dg` has `end`, `state`, `levels` and `run`.

## Astro Drop (4 Nov 2026)
- `reports/2026-11-04-arcade-astro-drop.html`: a merge-drop Arcade game, portrait (in short landscape it shows "Turn your phone upright to play"; the nightly LAYOUT test checks that notice at 780×360, as for Lighthouse, Archipelago and Echo Cave, since 9 Nov 2026). Built in the Lab and moved to Games on 6 Nov 2026 (Alec's call after reviewing the day's 29 runs; badge dropped, `dn:updated` 2026-11-06). Drop space objects into a field of light; two the same merge up the ladder (dust, junk, asteroid, Moon, Mercury, Mars, Earth, Neptune, Saturn, Jupiter, Sun); two Suns make a supernova that clears the field, pays 5,000 plus 250 per object and gives double points for 20 seconds. A run ends if an object stays above the line for 2 seconds. No rescue, but a meter banks a Comet (two at most) that merges with anything, the Sun included, so it can light a supernova. The player chooses when to use it, and the held piece comes back afterwards. Each Comet in a run costs more: 60 merge points, then 105, 150 and so on (4 Nov 2026 play test: every 18 made supernovas too easy; 80/140/200 until 5 Nov). **Bigger drops after a supernova** (6 Nov 2026, Alec: the field must still clear in full): each supernova, up to three, widens `pick()`'s drop range by a quarter of a size (`mx + 0.25×novas`), so runs with supernovas end a little sooner (the day's three-supernova runs took 13-17 minutes). A calibration step, not a redesign: high scores from earlier runs must stay beatable. Bots with a forced supernova: a good bot lasts 1.9 minutes after it, down from 2.3, and 1.5 after a second, down from 2.1. A whole size per supernova (live for an hour on 6 Nov) cut those to 1.4 and 0.85, which Alec judged too big a change. Play before the first supernova is unchanged. Calibration notes: `claude/astro-drop-calibration.md` in the Noodle Games project.
- **Round:** one run, free or Daily. `level` is the biggest object reached (1 to 11). Stars at 5,000 points or Saturn, 12,000 or Jupiter, 25,000 or the Sun (5 Nov 2026, Alec's call after the studio review); `win` means at least one star. The Daily is the same drop order for everyone (seeded by London day), one scored go, with a streak; it has no Board link yet.
- **Saves:** `digi-astro-drop-v1`, including settings, best, Atlas, stardust and rank, the Daily results and streak, and separate resumable free (`run`) and Daily (`drun`) runs. The world is simulated in field units, so phone size doesn't change the game. Seven field looks unlock by rank, with a rank-up moment on the end screen. Each object has its own merge voice (`VO`), the background chord moves every 14 seconds and opens up as bigger objects are reached, Earth's clouds drift and the Sun's halo breathes.
- `window.__dg` has `end`, plus `pair`, `fill`, `setBest` and `run` for tests and bot tuning.

- **Daily on the Board** (5 Nov 2026): `toolchain/sql/2026-11-05-game-daily-board.sql` adds `board.game_daily`, `board_game_submit` and `board_game_day`; existing puzzle tables and functions are untouched and these scores stay out of All puzzles and Records. `dn-track.js` loads `dn-board.js` on this one game page, which submits the Daily's `game_end`. `board.html` has an Astro Drop pill (`p=astro`). The app doesn't show it.
- First-run tips use `save.tips` bits; each shows once.

## Astro Drop: Starquake (Lab, 15 Nov 2026)
- `reports/2026-11-15-arcade-astro-drop-quake.html`: a Lab test copy of Astro Drop (`dn:badge` In development) for Alec to play-test a second bonus beside the Comet, so the live game, its Board Daily and everyone's bests stay untouched. Slug `astro-drop-quake`, save `digi-astro-drop-quake-v1`. On first open it copies the main game's progress (best, Atlas, stardust and rank, feats, records, medals, field look, settings) from `digi-astro-drop-v1`, never its runs, Daily or streak, and never writes back. Its filename doesn't match `-astro-drop.html`, so `dn-track.js` doesn't load the Board for it and its Daily isn't submitted; the end screen's board link is hidden. Back links go to the Lab.
- **Starquake:** earned by drops (Alec, 15 Nov 2026, after his first play: milestones came too early to be useful): the first at drop 60, then one 45 drops after each one earned, but never sooner than 50 seconds of play after the last (`Q1`, `QSTEP`, `QGAPT`, so fast random dropping can't farm them); two can be banked (`QMAX`). Comet drops don't count. Set off by a firm shake of the phone (three jolts over 16 m/s² within 0.65 s, `devicemotion`; Android needs no permission, iPhone asks on the first Play tap) or the orange button beside the Comet; a "Shake for Starquake" switch turns the shake off. Five pulses over 1.5 s (`QP`, strength 1 down to 0.4) throw every object up and hard sideways, small objects furthest (`QK`: up 1.8, side 3, sway .6), leaning the way the phone is tilted, and matching objects merge from further apart while it runs (`QK.gap` 1.45 against the usual 1.08). Friction and damping drop while it shakes, then heavy damping settles the pile. The top line is off from the shake until the pile is nearly still or 2.1 s after the shaking (about 2.5 to 3.5 s in all), then the usual 2 s grace applies. No drops meanwhile. Merges during it count as normal merges and can start chains, with no bonus of their own. Rumble, screen shake and a long buzz.
- **Bots** (15 Nov 2026, second version; one quake forced at 100 and 160 drops, about 20 each): 4 to 5 merges a quake (the first version made about 2, which Alec found too few), mean move 0.2 to 0.25 field widths, the highest object about 0.3 above the line, nothing left over the line. Whole runs: good 13,359 with about 3.3 quakes against 11,663 without; casual 10,348 with quakes against 6,581 without (so quakes narrow the gap between good and casual play); spam every 0.3 s with quakes 7,821 (before the 50-second gap it was 13,169, beating good play). `game_end` adds `quakes` and `qmerges`. `window.__dg` adds `quake()` and `qstate()`; `run()` takes `{quake:true}`.
